# 摘要

疫苗接种是预防和控制传染病最有效的公共卫生干预措施，但传统的人工管理模式存在预约效率低、库存管理不精准、信息孤岛、统计分析能力不足等问题。随着疫苗接种需求的不断增长和信息化技术的快速发展，开发一套高效、便捷、规范的疫苗预约接种管理系统具有重要的现实意义。

本课题设计并实现了一套基于Spring Boot与UniApp的疫苗预约接种管理系统。系统采用前后端分离架构，后端使用Java 17和Spring Boot 3.2作为开发框架，结合MyBatis-Plus实现数据持久化，前端使用UniApp跨端开发框架，可同时编译运行于iOS、Android、H5等多个平台。系统为管理员、医生、居民三端用户提供协同服务，涵盖用户管理、疫苗管理、接种点管理、库存管理、预约管理、接种核销、公告管理、统计报表等核心功能模块。

本系统的创新点主要体现在三个方面：一是实现了基于有效期优先（FEFO）的疫苗批次分配策略，确保疫苗按有效期先后使用，避免过期浪费；二是设计了预约与库存联动机制，预约时自动锁定库存、核销时自动扣减库存，通过乐观锁防止超卖；三是构建了完整的预约状态机模型，涵盖已预约、已签到、预检、接种、留观、已完成等10余种状态，实现了业务全流程的可追溯管理。

系统开发完成后，经过功能测试和性能测试验证，各项功能正常运行，核心接口响应时间满足业务需求，并发场景下数据一致性得到保障。系统已在模拟环境中部署运行，各项指标达到设计要求。

**关键词**：疫苗预约；接种管理；Spring Boot；UniApp；FEFO库存管理

# Abstract

Vaccination is one of the most effective public health interventions for preventing and controlling infectious diseases. However, traditional manual management approaches suffer from low appointment efficiency, imprecise inventory management, information silos, and insufficient statistical analysis capabilities. With the continuous growth of vaccination demand and the rapid development of information technology, developing an efficient, convenient, and standardized vaccine appointment and vaccination management system holds significant practical importance.

This thesis designs and implements a vaccine appointment and vaccination management system based on Spring Boot and UniApp. The system adopts a front-end and back-end separated architecture, with the back-end developed using Java 17 and Spring Boot 3.2, combined with MyBatis-Plus for data persistence. The front-end uses the UniApp cross-platform development framework, which can compile and run on iOS, Android, H5, and other platforms simultaneously. The system provides collaborative services for three types of users: administrators, doctors, and residents, covering core functional modules including user management, vaccine management, vaccination site management, inventory management, appointment management, vaccination verification, notice management, and statistical reporting.

The innovations of this system are mainly reflected in three aspects: First, it implements a batch allocation strategy based on First Expiry First Out (FEFO), ensuring vaccines are used in order of expiration date to avoid waste. Second, it designs a linkage mechanism between appointments and inventory, automatically locking inventory when appointments are made and automatically deducting inventory upon verification, using optimistic locking to prevent overselling. Third, it constructs a complete appointment state machine model, covering more than 10 states including appointment made, checked-in, pre-check, vaccination, observation, and completed, achieving full-process traceability management.

After development, the system was verified through functional and performance testing. All functions operate normally, core interface response times meet business requirements, and data consistency under concurrent scenarios is ensured. The system has been deployed in a simulation environment, and all indicators meet the design requirements.

**Key words**: Vaccine Appointment; Vaccination Management; Spring Boot; UniApp; FEFO Inventory Management
